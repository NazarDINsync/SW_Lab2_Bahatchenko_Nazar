### **1. The Foundations of Detroit Techno**
* **Origins and Pioneers:** Detroit techno emerged from early synth-pop influences (such as Kraftwerk, Yellow Magic Orchestra, Giorgio Moroder, Depeche Mode, and New Order) alongside funk influences (Parliament-Funkadelic). 
  * Pioneering group **Cybotron** (formed by **Juan Atkins**, **Rick Davis**, and **Jon-5**) released seminal tracks like *"Techno City"*. Atkins also established the label **Metroplex**.
  * The architects known as **The Belleville Three**—specifically **Derrick May** (also recording as **Rhythim Is Rhythim**, creator of *"Strings of Life"* and *"Nude Photo"*) and **Kevin Saunderson**—helped establish the genre alongside figures like **Blake Baxter** and **Chez Damier**.
* **Underground Resistance and Minimal Techno:** 
  * The collective **Underground Resistance (UR)**—featuring **Mike Banks**, **Jeff Mills**, and **Robert Hood**—advanced Detroit techno through releases like *Nation 2 Nation* and the *X-101/X-102/X103* series, also influencing artists like Dave Angel.
  * Producer **Richie Hawtin** expanded the techno framework through minimal techno, his project **Plastikman** (releasing albums such as *Sheet One* and *Musik*), and his work under the alias **F.U.S.E.**

### **2. Ambient Roots and Brian Eno**
* Ambient music emerged from classical, minimal music, industrial, and musique concrète traditions.
* **Brian Eno** (alongside avant-garde figures like John Cage) provided the foundational ambient lineage that directly fed into IDM and ambient electronic styles.

### **3. The Emergence of IDM and Intelligent Techno**
* The lineage of ambient music and techno converged into **intelligent dance music (IDM)** and **intelligent techno**:
  * **Aphex Twin** bridged Eno’s ambient lineage into IDM, releasing landmark records such as *Selected Ambient Works 85–92*, *Selected Ambient Works Volume II*, and the *Richard D. James Album*.
  * **Autechre** and Aphex Twin formed the core of pioneering IDM acts (later extending their influence to alternative rock artists like Thom Yorke and Radiohead on *Kid A*).
  * **Boards of Canada** (consisting of Mike Sandison, Marcus Eoin, and Chris Horne) expanded IDM and downtempo through albums such as *Music Has the Right to Children*, *Geogaddi*, and *The Campfire Headphase*.

### Development into IDM
* **Musical Roots:** Intelligent dance music (IDM) emerged as an experimental form of electronic music associated with early-1990s ambient techno, "electronic listening music," and electronica. It drew on electronic dance music styles such as **Detroit techno**, acid house, ambient techno, and breakbeat.
* **Evolution to Home Listening:** Following the late-1980s acid house and early rave scenes (where ambient house fused house pulses with ambient music), dance-oriented experimentation shifted toward post-rave music suited for home/sedentary listening (initially called terms like "ambient techno" and "armchair techno"). 
* **Coining of IDM:** The phrase "intelligent dance music" became associated with the 1992 compilation *Artificial Intelligence* (released by Warp) and became the most common term used for this experimental dance music during the mid-to-late 1990s.
